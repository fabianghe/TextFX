# Changelog

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-13

- Pop effect: letters grow and settle back to their size, one after another (Pulse also shrinks them).

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-13

- First release, extracted from VIRTUOUS's text highlights and made generic: TextFxAnimator (whole text and per-word styles), TextFxStyleSheet, TextFxMarkup (configurable tag), TextFxTypewriter.
- Effects: Wave, Bounce, Pulse, Shake, Shimmer.
